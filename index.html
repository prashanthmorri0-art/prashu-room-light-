How It Works

1

PIR Sensor

Detects movement when a person enters the room.

2

Controller

Receives the sensor signal and decides what to do.

3

Relay

Acts as an electronic switch for the bulb.

4

LED Bulb

Turns on only when motion is detected.

Benefits
